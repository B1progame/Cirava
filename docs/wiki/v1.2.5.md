# Cirava 1.2.5

The animated transfer route now cycles through seven file-icon styles: folders, images, documents, spreadsheets, videos, archives, and audio. It varies the packet spacing and pace, and splits the icons across upload and download directions when both are running. The route always uses three packet icons total.

Reduced-motion settings continue to suppress the moving packets.
