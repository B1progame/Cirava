# Cirava help

Cirava moves files between this PC and Google Drive. Pick the task you came here to do:

- [Get started](Getting-started): install Cirava and connect a Google account.
- [Set up Google Drive access](Google-Drive-setup): create the Google Cloud desktop client Cirava needs.
- [Upload files](Uploading): choose files, pick a Drive folder, and follow the transfer.
- [Download files or folders](Downloading): save Drive items to this PC.
- [Follow and control transfers](Transfers): read progress, pause or resume work, and use the system tray.
- [Fix a problem](Troubleshooting): work through sign-in, Drive, and transfer errors.
- [Privacy and account access](Privacy-and-security): understand what Cirava stores and what access it requests.

For development, see the repository's [architecture](https://github.com/B1progame/Cirava/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md), [testing guide](https://github.com/B1progame/Cirava/blob/main/TESTING.md), and [release process](https://github.com/B1progame/Cirava/blob/main/RELEASING.md).
